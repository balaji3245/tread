## 1. Executive Summary & Resolution

During Final Out-of-Sample (OOS) validation, an apparent signal-count discrepancy was observed:
* **Baseline Evaluated Signals / Trades**: `1,875`
* **H006 Evaluated Signal Opportunities**: `1,970`
* **Difference**: `+95` opportunities ($+5.07\%$)

This forensic audit inspected the exact execution loop in `entry_timing_engine.py` and `final_oos_engine.py`. 

### Key Audit Finding
The discrepancy is **NOT** due to a difference in signal generation, duplicate accounting, lookahead, or modified indicator code. The raw underlying signal generator produced **exactly 4,782 raw unconstrained signals** across the Final OOS dataset for both strategies.

The difference in evaluated opportunities is the direct, deterministic consequence of **Asymmetric Channel Occupancy under `max_concurrent_trades = 1`**:
* **Baseline** enters 100% of signals immediately and holds positions for an average of **18.2 minutes** (up to 60 minutes), locking the concurrency channel and skipping all subsequent raw signals during that holding window.
* **H006** enforces a tight 2-minute retracement window. When a setup fails to pull back within 2 minutes ($763\text{ times}$), H006 releases the concurrency slot at **minute +2** rather than being trapped in an active 18+ minute position.
* Because H006 is idle more frequently, it encounters and evaluates **95 additional raw signal opportunities** that baseline was blocked from seeing.
